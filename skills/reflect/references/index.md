# PhilosophAI Reference Bundle

The knowledge bundle behind the `reflect` skill. Each concept is one
markdown file with OKF frontmatter (`type`, `title`, `description`, `tags`).

# System

* [The Scribe Protocol](journal.md) - Memory files, journal entry format, and first-run onboarding.
* [Balance Coach](coach.md) - Meta-lens: journal pattern analysis and next-lens recommendations.

# Philosophers — Ancient & Classical

* [Socrates](socrates.md) - The Gadfly: exposes assumptions through relentless questioning; never answers.
* [Aristotle](aristotle.md) - The Virtue Navigator: habits, character, and the Golden Mean.
* [Epicurus](epicurus.md) - The Tranquility Guide: simple pleasures, fewer desires, less anxiety.
* [Seneca](seneca.md) - The Stoic Compass: resilience through the dichotomy of control.
* [Nagarjuna](nagarjuna.md) - The Void Seer: deconstructs rigid concepts; impermanence and non-attachment.
* [Confucius](confucius.md) - The Harmony Architect: roles, ritual propriety, and ethical communication.
* [Lao Tzu](laotzu.md) - The Sage of Flow: effortless action when striving becomes forcing.

# Philosophers — Modern

* [Montaigne](montaigne.md) - The Honest Essayist: humane self-examination and the patron saint of journaling.
* [Kant](kant.md) - The Ethical Duty Compass: universalizable maxims; persons as ends, never mere means.
* [Mill](mill.md) - The Consequence Weigher: utilitarian tradeoffs and higher pleasures.
* [Kierkegaard](kierkegaard.md) - The Knight of Commitment: anxiety as freedom's dizziness; the leap to commit.
* [Nietzsche](nietzsche.md) - The Path to Overcoming: self-mastery, amor fati, suffering as fuel.

# Philosophers — Contemporary

* [Dewey](dewey.md) - The Pragmatic Problem-Solver: life's dilemmas as experiments.
* [Camus](camus.md) - The Absurdist Rebel: defiant joy against a silent universe.
* [de Beauvoir](debeauvoir.md) - The Authenticity Check: radical freedom against bad faith.
* [Nussbaum](nussbaum.md) - The Capability Catalyst: emotions as intelligence; flourishing as capability.
* [bell hooks](hooks.md) - The Liberatory Love Guide: love as practice; bias and power made visible.
